Sarah Dose & Ben Pillman

For a number of years I’ve wanted to do a podcast, because I like the idea of creating, editing and then publishing – be that written (typed?) or spoken word. Hence this (inconsistently published) blog.<br>

But I think I need to throw in the towel before I’ve even begun…

The reason?

Google Notebook LM can now summarise articles, and enable you to query and summarise – like any other LLM. But they also produce an audio version, with two American hosts, discussing the topic you are investigating.

So I thought I’d give it a try, using the recent GPhC proposed changes to the guidance on providing pharmacy services at a distance, including on the internet.

So I will hand over to my co-hosts, who I’ve named<br>
Ben Pillman & Sarah Dose.

Listen to Online Pharmacies – GPhC by PharmacistDaz on [SoundCloud](https://on.soundcloud.com/U7GMY).
