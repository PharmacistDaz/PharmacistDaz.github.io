I thought I'd share some of the podcasts that I'm currently listening to, or I've stumbled across recently. I'm happy to have some pointers for Podcasts you think I might enjoy or even stretch my usual repertoire.

---

I thought I'd also share what I'm using to listen to the pods - mainly on my android mobile.

- PocketCasts - this had been my go to app and I even paid for the premium version. Recently the price has increased dramatically so I'm looking at alternatives. https://pocketcasts.com/
- Google Podcasts - to be honest I'm not impressed with the Google app, and don't like the search function or the UI. I suspect it won't last long on my mobile.
- Podbean - this is my newest download, and so far I'm quite impressed. Finding podcast and even some of the recommended ones are spot on. https://bit.ly/podbean_recommend
