A story caught my attention this week. Thousands of NHS patients were exposed to potential infection risk during surgical procedures because a supplier failed to adequately document calibrations for sealing equipment and sterilisation sensors.

Not a deliberate act of negligence – but a failure of process assurance. 150 NHS trusts. A mass recall. Disruption across the system at a time when the NHS can least afford it.
